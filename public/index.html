Dr. Sturgis

My Profile

Set up your personal practice profile. These are self-selected preferences, not a diagnosis. Everything stays in this browser.

Practice Paths

Each visit you can continue the last activity or pick any speaking activity for your age group. These are practice ideas, not treatment, and nothing here scores how fluent you sound.

Live Conversation

Talk with a friendly AI partner in a turn-based conversation: you speak, the AI replies, then it listens again. Voice features send audio and transcripts to Google Gemini only after you consent.

Speech Insights

Informal, transcript-based observations about your own sessions, never compared with other people. Every number is an experimental estimate, not a clinical measurement. Transcript-based analysis cannot reliably detect speech blocks, sound prolongations, tension, or every disfluency, and speech-recognition transcripts sometimes clean up what was actually said. Nothing here is a clinical measurement, a diagnosis, or a score compared against other people.

Progress

Your practice history, weekly summaries and a report you can voluntarily share with your speech-language pathologist.

All personal data stays in this browser (localStorage) unless you explicitly send it for AI processing. Audio and transcripts may be shared with Google Gemini when you use recording or conversation features.

Before you record anything

Dr. Sturgis is a student prototype — not a medical device and not a substitute for professional care.

  • 🎙️ If you use voice features, your audio and transcripts are sent to Google Gemini, an external AI service, to get a reply or transcript.
  • 🧠 AI output is for practice and reflection only — it is not a diagnosis, not a clinical measurement, and not treatment advice.
  • 🧒 For children, a caregiver should help set up and supervise. We never ask for a child's name, age in years, or other identifying details — only an age group.
  • 🗑️ You can export or delete all your data at any time from the Privacy section.

You can browse without recording. Recording and live conversation stay disabled until you agree.
